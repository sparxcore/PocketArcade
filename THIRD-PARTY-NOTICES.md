PocketArcade may use or distribute third-party software, example code, build tools, documentation, fonts, icons, audio, images, or other materials.

Those materials remain subject to their own licence terms. The PocketArcade licence does not replace, override, or relicense third-party material.

## Maintainer requirements

Before adding a dependency or copied material:

1. Record its name, version, source, copyright holder, and licence.
2. Confirm that its licence is compatible with the intended PocketArcade distribution model.
3. Include any required licence text, attribution, source offer, or notice.
4. Keep third-party files clearly identifiable.
5. Do not remove upstream copyright notices.
6. Obtain approval before adding strong-copyleft, network-copyleft, source-available, noncommercial, or otherwise restrictive dependencies.
